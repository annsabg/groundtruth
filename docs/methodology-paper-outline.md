# Methodology Paper — Outline

Target: *Acta Astronautica* or *Journal of Space Safety Engineering*.
Not written in v0.1 — scoped only.

1. **Problem** — the three waste streams (operational knowledge waste,
   research comparability waste, research registry gap), positioned
   against existing literature.
2. **Related work** — INTERACT GIS, ESA Topical Team review on Space
   Analogs and Behavioral Health (npj Microgravity, 2024), Bell et al.
   (2019) systematic review of team dynamics in analog environments.
3. **Method** — schema design principles: confidence ratings over false
   precision, pseudonymization approach, contribution/review workflow,
   why SQLite + per-record JSON.
4. **Case study** — v0.1 seed data (FMARS Crews 15–18, Mars160): what
   patterns emerged, what the registry surfaced that narrative reports
   alone didn't.
5. **Limitations and future work** — website/dashboard, broader sector
   adoption, governance model for multi-contributor review.

full context this outline was drafted against.
